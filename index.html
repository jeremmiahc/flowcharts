How to Trade This With Order Flow

Gamma gives the level. Order flow gives the trigger.

Use the gamma map to know where reactions are likely. Then use your actual execution tools — Bookmap, CVD, footprint, absorption, and failed breaks — to decide whether the level is holding or failing.

Near Call Wall

Look for absorption, failed breakout, CVD divergence, or trapped buyers. A clean acceptance above can shift into expansion.

Near Put Wall

Look for absorption, failed breakdown, CVD divergence, or trapped sellers. If the wall fails, downside can expand.

Near Gamma Flip

Expect behavior to change. Do not blindly fade. Wait for CVD and Bookmap confirmation.

Positive Gamma

Expect more mean reversion, pinning, and chop. Be careful chasing breakouts in the middle.

Negative Gamma

Expect more expansion, momentum, and larger moves. Breaks can travel farther than expected.
